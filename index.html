WHY THIS IS AI, NOT AUTOMATION

Judgment sits between the signal and the action.

Rules enforce policy. A locally trained Naive Bayes diagnosis model interprets event context — issuer state, payment method, invoice age and dispute indicators — to recommend the right intervention.

Low-confidence cases never auto-act. They are routed to a human with the evidence already assembled. The demo reports held-out accuracy separately from recovery outcomes.
