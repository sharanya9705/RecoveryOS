WHY THIS IS AI, NOT AUTOMATION

Judgment sits between the signal and the action.

Rules enforce policy. A lightweight diagnosis model interprets multi-event context — issuer downtime, customer retry intent, payment method, invoice age, consent and prior contact — to recommend the right intervention.

Low-confidence cases never auto-act. They are routed to a human with the evidence already assembled.
